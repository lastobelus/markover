The Tiny Story

The auth fixture is a road map. Slice 3 borrows only the ordinary route—open, save, get, edit—and drives it with the actual packaged car. It does not rerun the fixture's break-in drills, and it does not crash the car to measure how much luggage survives.

A pass means one already-saved review survived a normal packaged-app restart and remained usable through the real CLI contract. It says nothing broader about hostile callers or bounded-loss behavior.
