# Markover Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction rather than promising dates. The public
[Markover Announcement Readiness project](https://github.com/users/lastobelus/projects/3)
is the live source of truth for status, while
[issue 5](https://github.com/lastobelus/markover/issues/5) defines the release
gates.

## Focused preview

The [Focused preview milestone](https://github.com/lastobelus/markover/milestone/2)
prepares a candid early macOS preview for a small group of agent-tool users. It
focuses on:

- secure application, renderer, and loopback-service boundaries;
- repeatable Apple Silicon and Intel installation and release validation;
- dependable interpretation of reviewer annotations;
- accurate privacy, storage, deletion, support, and compatibility guidance;
- continuous correctness checks and honest public launch materials; and
- a controlled first feedback round.

## Broad announcement

The [Broad announcement milestone](https://github.com/lastobelus/markover/milestone/1)
is Markover's broad-launch phase. In addition to the focused-preview gate, it
requires:

- Developer ID signing, notarization, provenance, and rollback guidance;
- accessible keyboard and VoiceOver workflows;
- understandable review-history controls and Markdown compatibility limits;
- contributor, conduct, and private security-reporting surfaces; and
- staged launch operations with support and rollback coverage.

## Later

Possible later directions include richer Markdown and review compatibility,
longer-term review lifecycle and integration work, and broader platform
evaluation. These are themes, not commitments.

Running a development checkout on Linux may work but is currently untested and
unsupported. Windows is likely to require additional path and startup work.
Official binaries and ongoing support for either platform would require funded
sponsorship or a second maintainer who owns implementation and continuing
platform support. Serious proposals should begin in
[Ideas](https://github.com/lastobelus/markover/discussions/categories/ideas).
