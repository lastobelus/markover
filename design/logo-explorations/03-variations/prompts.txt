COMMON PROMPT

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Markover logo and favicon variation board
Input images: Image 1 is the parent identity reference. Preserve its core identity
grammar: two adjacent vertical panes, the right pane reading as a folded-corner
document, plus a clean horizontal wordmark and matching square favicon.
Subject: show exactly three coordinated elements on one clean board: large standalone
mark, horizontal lockup, compact square favicon/app icon.
Style/medium: crisp flat vector-logo aesthetic, precise negative space, no mockup
scene.
Text (verbatim): “Markover” — spell exactly M-A-R-K-O-V-E-R.
Constraints: preserve the recognizable two-pane folded-document construction and
family resemblance; exact spelling; favicon must use the same mark; flat colors;
crisp edges; legible at small size.
Avoid: redesigning into an unrelated symbol, extra icons, gradients, shadows, 3D,
photography, stationery, captions, slogans, watermarks, and extraneous text.

02 — WARM EDITORIAL

Create a warm editorial interpretation. Make the left pane about 42% and the right
folded pane about 58% of the mark width, slightly shorter and broader than the
reference. Use burnt terracotta, deep burgundy, warm parchment, and a dark espresso
wordmark. Set “Markover” in a sophisticated low-contrast contemporary slab serif,
avoiding a vintage-Western feel.

03 — NORDIC GREEN

Create a restrained Scandinavian variation with panes about 20% taller and 18%
narrower than the reference, more air between them, and a small precise fold. Use
deep pine green, fresh mint, near-black blue, and cool off-white. Set “Markover” in
an open humanist sans with softly flared terminals and generous tracking. Avoid leaf
imagery and environmental-brand clichés.

04 — MONOCHROME WIDE

Create an austere black-and-ivory Swiss variation. Make the mark roughly 35% wider
and 18% shorter, with a 55:45 pane-width ratio, generous internal gap, and a bold
triangular fold cutout. Use carbon black and warm ivory only. Set “Markover” in a
precise medium-weight, slightly extended neo-grotesk with tight refined kerning.

05 — PLUM + TANGERINE

Create a friendlier geometric variation with equal-width, medium-height panes. Round
the two lower outer corners and the upper-left corner of the left pane while keeping
the fold triangular and sharp. Use saturated aubergine, vivid tangerine, deep plum,
and pale lilac-white. Set “Markover” in a medium-bold rounded geometric sans with
circular counters and slightly loose tracking. Avoid a sticky-note appearance.

06 — OCEAN TECHNICAL

Create a composed developer-tool variation with an asymmetric 65:35 colored-width
ratio, medium-height panes, a thin deliberate channel, and a compact fold cutout.
Use dark petrol teal, luminous cyan-aqua, very dark navy, and icy off-white. Set
“Markover” in a medium-weight semi-condensed technical grotesk with subtly squared
curves. Avoid ocean imagery and coding brackets.

07 — DARK MODE

Create a high-contrast dark-mode variation with equal-width panes about 25% shorter
and broader than the reference, a medium gap, and a slightly oversized fold cutout.
Use warm white for the left pane, bright coral-red for the right pane, warm-white
type, and a midnight navy background. Set “Markover” in a bold semi-condensed
grotesk with tall x-height and tight spacing. Avoid neon, glow, and glass effects.

08 — OLIVE MONO

Create a publishing-meets-code variation with broad, almost-square panes, the right
pane slightly narrower, a generous gap, and a shallow clipped fold. Use dark olive,
acid chartreuse, deep ink, and warm off-white. Set “Markover” in a medium-weight
contemporary monospaced sans with slightly expanded tracking. Avoid raw Markdown
characters, terminal UI, and military styling.
