# Concepts 07–08

## 07 — Continuous handoff

**Rationale:** A single ribbon moves through an `m`, revision loop, and return/check
gesture. The continuity suggests feedback surviving the handoff from reviewer to
agent. Navy and sky blue deliberately move beyond the current coral product palette.

**Generation prompt**

> Use case: logo-brand
>
> Asset type: brand identity exploration board for a desktop Markdown review app
>
> Primary request: Create concept 07 for the brand “Markover”: a refined continuous-loop logo expressing revision, handoff, and “mark over” through one unbroken ribbon that folds into an abstract lowercase m and a subtle check/return gesture. It should feel quietly premium, humane, and contemporary—not corporate SaaS.
>
> Scene/backdrop: clean flat warm-white presentation board, no environmental mockups
>
> Subject: show three coordinated elements with generous spacing: a large standalone symbol; a horizontal lockup with the exact word “Markover”; and a compact square favicon/app-icon version
>
> Style/medium: crisp flat vector-logo aesthetic, elegant monoline/ribbon geometry with intelligent negative space; custom neo-humanist sans wordmark
>
> Composition/framing: orderly identity board, front-facing, large legible assets, no decorative captions or tiny body text
>
> Color palette: midnight navy, soft sky blue, warm ivory; maximum three colors
>
> Text (verbatim): “Markover”
>
> Constraints: exact spelling; symbol must remain distinctive at 16px; favicon should simplify the same core symbol; balanced optical geometry; no gradients, shadows, 3D, or photography
>
> Avoid: generic infinity symbol, generic chain link, cloud logo, swoosh, pen nib, checkmark-in-a-circle, trademark symbols, stationery or device mockups, extraneous words

## 08 — Overprint layers

**Rationale:** The source document and review layer overlap to reveal an `M` and
annotation aperture. The risograph palette gives Markover a cultured,
independent-publishing character rather than a conventional developer-tool look.

**Generation prompt**

> Use case: logo-brand
>
> Asset type: brand identity exploration board for a desktop Markdown review app
>
> Primary request: Create concept 08 for the exact brand “Markover”: an experimental art-publishing identity built around overprinting and layered reading. Two offset document/block shapes overlap to create a surprising abstract M and a small eye/annotation aperture in negative space, expressing source text plus reviewer layer. Distinctive, cultured, memorable, slightly unconventional.
>
> Scene/backdrop: clean pale recycled-paper presentation board, no real-world mockups
>
> Subject: show three coordinated elements: large standalone symbol, horizontal wordmark with exact text, and compact square favicon/app icon
>
> Style/medium: flat vector-friendly risograph/overprint language with precise geometric shapes; subtle printed-registration character but clean enough for software; custom wide serif or sharp editorial wordmark
>
> Composition/framing: sparse gallery-like identity board, front-facing, generous margins, assets large and legible; no captions
>
> Color palette: saturated ultramarine and hot orange-red over pale paper, with dark plum where colors overlap
>
> Materials/textures: mostly flat color, only a very light screenprint grain; hard readable edges
>
> Text (verbatim): “Markover”
>
> Constraints: exact spelling, symbol must work in one-color silhouette and simplify cleanly at 16px, favicon uses the same core mark, maximum three inks
>
> Avoid: CMYK printer cliché, literal eye icon, literal sheets of paper with writing, Venn diagram, generic stacked squares, gradients, shadows, 3D, photography, stationery mockups, watermark, extraneous words
