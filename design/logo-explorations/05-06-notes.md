# Markover logo explorations 05–06

Both boards were generated as `logo-brand` explorations with the built-in image-generation tool. They are concept boards rather than production vector masters; the selected direction should be redrawn and optically refined in SVG before release.

## 05 — Sticky fold

**Rationale.** A rounded sticky-note/page silhouette makes review and annotation immediately legible, while the aubergine folded corner meets the yellow page to create a small `M` in negative space. The palette feels warm and product-led, and the compact page-plus-M construction has a credible path to a simple small-size app icon.

**Palette.** Lemon yellow, deep aubergine, warm white.

**Generated asset.** `05-sticky-fold.png`

**Prompt.**

> Use case: logo-brand  
> Asset type: clean landscape identity presentation board for a software product  
> Primary request: Create an original friendly visual identity concept for “Markover”, a small Electron app for reviewing Markdown as a structural tree, attaching block-level feedback, and producing copyable agent handoff. The core idea is a playful sticky note or paper page with a folded corner and an annotation tab that subtly forms a capital M in the negative space.  
> Scene/backdrop: flat warm-white presentation board, no scene and no mockup environment  
> Subject: show exactly three coordinated identity applications: (1) a large standalone logo symbol, (2) a horizontal logo lockup with symbol and wordmark, and (3) a small square favicon/app-icon crop designed to stay clear at 16px. Arrange these as a disciplined graphic-design presentation with generous whitespace.  
> Style/medium: vector-friendly flat logo design, rounded geometric construction, product-led and approachable but not childish; precise curves, simple silhouette, optical balance  
> Composition/framing: landscape brand concept board, large symbol on the left, horizontal lockup to the upper right, favicon/app-icon crop to the lower right; clean aligned grid  
> Color palette: lemon yellow, deep aubergine, and warm white only; high contrast  
> Text (verbatim): “Markover” — render exactly once, with exact spelling M-a-r-k-o-v-e-r, in a clean rounded contemporary sans serif. No other text anywhere.  
> Constraints: the folded corner or annotation tab must subtly form an M; strong unmistakable silhouette; favicon must remain recognizable at 16px; original design only; flat colors; professional identity exploration; crisp edges; show no more than the three requested applications  
> Avoid: lifestyle or stationery mockups, paper texture, shadows, gradients, 3D effects, UI screenshots, extra labels, taglines, random letters, watermarks, clip art, childish mascot styling, overly complex detail, thin strokes, tiny text

## 06 — Brutalist stamp

**Rationale.** A massive condensed `M` supplies a forceful toolmaker silhouette, while one vermilion diagonal acts as both editorial strike-through and decisive review gesture. The condensed wordmark and near-monochrome system make this direction feel independent, direct, and unlike conventional friendly productivity branding.

**Palette.** Raw black, vermilion red, pale newsprint.

**Generated asset.** `06-brutalist-stamp.png`

**Prompt.**

> Use case: logo-brand  
> Asset type: clean landscape identity presentation board for a software product  
> Primary request: Create an original radical brutalist visual identity concept for “Markover”, a small Electron app for reviewing Markdown as a structural tree, attaching block-level feedback, and producing copyable agent handoff. The core symbol is a chunky block capital M visibly overwritten or cut through by one decisive diagonal review stroke, like an editor’s correction mark or approval stamp.  
> Scene/backdrop: flat newsprint-colored presentation board, no scene and no mockup environment  
> Subject: show exactly three coordinated identity applications: (1) a large standalone blocky M review-stamp symbol, (2) a horizontal logo lockup with symbol and wordmark, and (3) a small square favicon/app-icon crop designed to stay clear at 16px. Arrange these as a bold disciplined graphic-design specimen with generous negative space.  
> Style/medium: vector-friendly flat logo system, sharp brutalist geometry, chunky condensed sans-serif wordmark, raw editorial energy, rebellious independent toolmaker attitude, intentionally forceful but fully legible  
> Composition/framing: landscape brand concept board, oversized symbol on the left, horizontal lockup to the upper right, favicon/app-icon crop to the lower right; strict asymmetric grid  
> Color palette: raw black, vivid vermilion red, and pale newsprint only; extremely high contrast  
> Text (verbatim): “Markover” — render exactly once, exact spelling M-a-r-k-o-v-e-r, in heavy condensed type. No other text anywhere.  
> Constraints: the M must be unmistakable; exactly one thick diagonal vermilion review stroke should cross or notch the black M; favicon must reduce to a bold M plus slash and remain recognizable at 16px; original design only; flat colors; crisp hard edges; professional identity exploration; no more than the three requested applications  
> Avoid: distressed or eroded illegibility, grunge texture, lifestyle or stationery mockups, stamp-on-paper mockup effects, shadows, gradients, 3D effects, UI screenshots, extra labels, taglines, random letters, watermarks, thin strokes, fussy detail, rounded friendly startup styling

- Each board shows a standalone mark, horizontal wordmark, and favicon/app-icon treatment.
